Tap the 4 words where Belloc ended his lines.Draw a line after the 4 words where Belloc ended his lines.

The poem breaks after the word you tap. Tap it again to join it back. Read it aloud and listen for the rhymes, like heard and bird in Ed Vere's poem.

The Lion, the Lion, he dwells in the waste, He has a big head and a very small waist; But his shoulders are stark, and his jaws they are grim, And a good little child will not play with him.

Hilaire Belloc, The Lion (1896)

the waste = wild, empty land · stark = strong and bony · grim = fierce and frightening
