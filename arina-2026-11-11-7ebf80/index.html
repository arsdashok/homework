A little practice from our conversation. About 10 minutes.

1 · After have / has

Type the third form of the verb in the gap.

Like thisI’ve never ridden (ride) a horse. (ride, rode, ridden)
1

Have you ever (swim) the 50 metres butterfly in a competition?

2

Max has never (fly) on a plane: he gets nervous at airports.

3

Have you ever (see) a dolphin in the sea?

4

Our teacher has (write) the questions for the school olympiad.

5

We have already (take) three tests this week, and it’s only Wednesday.

2 · The …-est or the most …

Type only the missing word: “the” is already there.

Like thischeap → the cheapest  ·  expensive → the most expensive
1

The (hard) part of the trip was on the second day: we got up at five.

2

It was the (difficult) task in the whole olympiad.

3

Friday was the (busy) day of my week: a colloquium and a long training session.
