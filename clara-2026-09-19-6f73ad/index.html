Maths rounding on a number line
Like this: round 246,300 to the nearest 10,000. 1. Neighbours: 240,000 and 250,000.   2. Halfway: 245,000.
3. Each small step is 1,000, so 246,300 sits just past the first step after 245,000. It is past halfway, so it rounds to 250,000.
