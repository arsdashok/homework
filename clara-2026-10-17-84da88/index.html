Compensate

Bonds

The parts make a round number.

3,600 + 6,400 → 600 + 400 makes a thousand → 10,000

Count up

The two numbers are close.

9,004 − 8,995 → 5 to 9,000, then 4 more → 9

Known facts

A small fact does the work.

34 + 52 = 86, so 34,000 + 52,000 = 86,000

Same difference (for take away)

Change both numbers by the same amount so the number you take away is round. The gap stays the same.

9,005 − 3,998 → add 2 to both → 9,007 − 4,000 = 5,007
