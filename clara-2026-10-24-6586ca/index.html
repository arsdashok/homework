MathsMore or fewer, then altogether
Remember

“More” or “fewer” finds the second amount. “Altogether” needs one more step.

Step 1 finds the second amount, ★. Step 2 adds both amounts together.

Worked example

Farm A has 2,600 sheep. Farm B has 850 fewer. How many sheep are there altogether?

Step 1 Farm B = 2,600 − 850 = 1,750 ★

Step 2 Altogether = 2,600 + 1,750 = 4,350
