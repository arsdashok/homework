MathsSquare and cube numbers
Remember

A square number: a number times itself. 6² = 6 × 6 = 36. Say it: six squared.

A cube number: times itself, then times itself again. 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.

The little 2 never means × 2. The little 3 never means × 3.
