1 · Countries and cities

Choose the or no the for each gap.

No the with most countries and cities: France, Italy, Berlin, London.
The with names that have Kingdom, States, Republic or Emirates in them, and with plural names: the Czech Republic, the United Arab Emirates, the Maldives.
