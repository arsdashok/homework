253,700 to the nearest 10,000

  1. The ten thousands digit: 253,700
  2. Smaller neighbour: keep every digit up to and including that one. The rest turn into zeros: 250,000
  3. Bigger neighbour: ten thousand more: 260,000
  4. Halfway: ignore the thousands. 250 and 260, so 255. Bring the thousands back: 255,000
  5. 253,700 is before halfway, so round down.
  6. 253,700 rounds to 250,000
