AMy metaphors

A metaphor describes something by saying it is something else.

Example: a goalkeeper

I am the wall
because nothing gets past me.

That is true of a wall and of a goalkeeper: nothing gets past either.

Write three pairs of lines about yourself, like the example. Choose anything you do, and a thing that does the same action.

A new thing each time, not the wall or the speaker. Name a thing, not yourself.

BImagery

Imagery is words that paint a picture using the five senses: see, hear, touch, smell, taste.

Imagery is not onomatopoeia. Onomatopoeia is a word that copies a sound, like bang or buzz.

Example, from “July” by Ted Kooser and Connie Wanek

The darkness filled with blinking stars
like whispers for my eyes.

This one makes you see it.
