B · Make it a metaphor

Write the whole line again, with a thing instead of the describing word.

Example

I’m tall because I can reach the top shelf.

I am a ladder because I can reach the top shelf.

A ladder can reach the top shelf, and so can I. The because part is true of both.

The weirder the thing, the better.
