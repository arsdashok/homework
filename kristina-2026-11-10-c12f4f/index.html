Part 1 · Phrases from the book

Read the eight phrases and what they mean.

Tap 🔈 to hear a phrase.

  • to cope (with something)🔈to manage something difficult and not give up
  • to treasure something🔈to keep something carefully, because it is very special to you
  • I doubt (that / if …)🔈I don’t think it is true, or I don’t think it will happen The b is silent.
  • to speak your mind🔈to say openly what you really think
  • to accept a present (an invitation)🔈to take a present, or to say yes to an invitation
  • to make your way (to / down …)🔈to go somewhere: make your way down the stairs = go down the stairs
  • a lump in my throat🔈a tight feeling in your throat when you are about to cry
  • the hustle and bustle (of the city)🔈the noise and busy movement of a lot of people The t in bustle is silent.
