Words from the book

  • she used to treasure them🔈used to + a verb: she did it in the past, but not now. To treasure: to love something because it is special
  • built of stone🔈made of stone, like a house, a wall or a bridge
  • narrow🔈not wide
  • unlike everyone else🔈different from all the other people
  • bushes🔈low plants with lots of small branches, like small trees
  • an extension🔈a new part built onto a house
