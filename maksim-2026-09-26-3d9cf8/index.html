01

told me to or said

Your question from the lesson.

An instruction: tell + person + to + verb. News or a fact: said + the words, in quotes or after that.

Taxi driver: “Close your window. It’s cold today.” → He told me to close my window. He said, “It’s cold today.”

Report it in one sentence.

From your acting class.

must + verb, with no to. For the past, use had to + verb.

The lift was broken, so we had to walk to the ninth floor.
