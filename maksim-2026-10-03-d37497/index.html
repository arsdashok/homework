01

Report it

Statements, instructions and questions.

A statement: said (that) + past. An instruction: told me to + verb. A question: asked if + past (can → could, is → was).

At the hotel I asked: “Is breakfast included?” → I asked if breakfast was included.

Write what they said in one full sentence.

02

In your own words

Three forms that still slip.

must has no past form. Nothing already means “not”, so the verb stays positive. himself is for actions done to yourself: he cut himself.

Answer each one in a full sentence.

03

New words

From the lesson.

until = up to the moment when something happens. while = during the same time.

a burglary = someone gets into a place to steal when nobody is there. a robbery = someone takes things from a person, often with threats.

We sat in the café until the rain stopped. I listen to podcasts while I cook. It took me ages to find a parking space.

9. Last month two men in masks stopped a woman in the street and took her bag. It was a (robbery / burglary). Last night someone got into the empty office next to mine through a window and took two laptops. It was a (robbery / burglary).

10. Write one true sentence about your week. Use It took me ages to + verb.
