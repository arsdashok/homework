01

Report it

A statement, an instruction or a question.

Direct speech uses the exact words in quotation marks. Reported speech has no quotation marks. told me to + verb is always reported speech.

Statement: My brother: “I’m tired.” → My brother said (that) he was tired.

Instruction: The doctor: “Drink more water.” → The doctor told me to drink more water.

Question: The waiter: “Is the café open?” → The waiter asked if the café was open.

Time words change too: today → that day.
