One small word in a question

Have / Has+ been, finished, had… (with yet, ever, just). With been, use have or has, never was, were or did. Has with he, she, it.
Did+ a verb, for a finished time (yesterday, on Monday)
Was / Were+ an -ing verb or an adjective (working, late)
Do / Does+ a verb: habits now
