Exercise 1: at the farm

Choose the right words for each gap.

Look for the time words first.

1

Grandpa is a farmer. at six o’clock every morning.

He gets up  /  He’s getting up
2

Shelly loves films. a film every Friday evening.

She watches  /  She’s watching
3

Look at Cameron! in his clown’s costume.

He dances  /  He’s dancing
4

Look at the cows! grass in the field right now.

They eat  /  They’re eating
5

Gracie loves books. every day.

She reads  /  She’s reading
6

Shh! Look at the wild dog pup. at the moment.

It sleeps  /  It’s sleeping
7

Harry loves the garden. Grandpa there now.

He helps  /  He’s helping
8

Grandpa loves music. to the cows sometimes.

He sings  /  He’s singing
