Two phrases and a short recommendation

About 15 minutes. You can use your vocabulary page for help.

1. I found myself…

I found myself somewhere, or doing something = it happened, but I didn't plan it.
Does the phrase fit each situation? Choose fits or does not fit.
1.

I went out to buy bread and met a neighbour on the way. An hour later I found myself at a street festival.

2.

I booked a table for nine o'clock. At nine I found myself at the restaurant.

3.

I wanted to see the new exhibition at the Prado, so I went there on Sunday morning. I found myself at the Prado.

4.

After work we went for one quick coffee and kept talking. At midnight we found ourselves in a tapas bar on the other side of the city.

5.

Write 2-3 sentences about something that happened to you. Use I found myself or we found ourselves at least once.

2. Take part in

Check sentences 1 and 2. Write the corrected sentence, or write correct if there is no mistake.
1.

Last year our company took part in a big insurance conference in Barcelona.

2.

Next month I'd like to take part in in a Spanish cooking class.

3.

Now write one sentence of your own with take part in: something you have done, or would like to do.

3. A short recommendation

Write 3 sentences, one for each point below, to an English-speaking acquaintance who is coming to Spain. Recommend a food experience you know, in Madrid or anywhere in Spain. It can be a dish, a place or a kind of meal.
What to try, with one practical detail: where, when, what to order or the price Why you recommend it A question for your reader
