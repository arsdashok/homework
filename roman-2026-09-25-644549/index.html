I’d rather…

About 8 minutes. You can use your vocabulary page for help.

A friend’s plan

«Let’s go to the cinema tonight.»
I’d rather watch a film at home. I’m a bit tired.
After I’d rather, use a verb, without to: I’d rather go, not I’d rather to go or I’d rather quiet.
In 1-3, reply for yourself with I’d rather and a short reason. In 4 and 5, ask a friend with Would you rather … or …?
