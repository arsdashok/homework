I’d rather…

About 20 minutes.

1. Answer your friend

Your friend suggests a plan. Answer with I’d rather + a verb, and give a short reason.
«Let’s go to the cinema tonight.» → I’d rather watch a film at home. I’m tired.
No to after I’d rather.
1.

«Let’s order pizza tonight.»

2.

«Let’s have lunch at the office today.»

3.

«Let’s meet for dinner at six on Thursday.»

2. Ask your friend a question

Write a question with Would you rather … or …?
(walk / take the bus) → Would you rather walk or take the bus?
1.

Your next business trip: take the train or fly

2.

Sunday lunch: an expensive restaurant or an affordable café
