I prefer… It’s worth…

About 20 minutes.

1. Answer the question

Answer with I prefer + -ing, and add a short reason.
«What do you prefer doing on holiday?» → I prefer visiting small towns. They’re quieter than big cities.
1.

«What do you prefer doing on a free Sunday in Madrid?»

2. Ask your friend

Write each question. Use the frame in the item.
(walk / take the bus to work) → Do you prefer walking or taking the bus to work?
2.

(cook at home / eat out on a Friday evening) → Do you prefer …ing … or …ing …?

3.

(take a taxi to the airport) → Is it worth …ing …?

3. Give your friend a tip

A friend from Moscow is coming to Madrid for a weekend.

Write one sentence with It’s worth / It isn’t worth + -ing, and say why.
A friend is going to Valencia. → It’s worth trying the paella there. It’s much better than in Madrid.
4.

Something your friend should do.

5.

Something that is not a good idea on a short visit.

4. A message to a new colleague

You are starting a project with a new colleague in Spain.

Write 3-4 sentences to your colleague.
  • how you usually prefer working (call or write? start early or late?)
  • one tip about working with Spanish clients
  • one question for your colleague

Check: I prefer + -ing · It’s worth + -ing · a question
