Could you tell me…?

About 20 minutes.

1. Polite questions

Make each question polite. Start with Could you tell me or Do you know, and end with a question mark.
Example Where is the lift? → Could you tell me where the lift is?
When does the shop open? → Do you know when the shop opens?
1.

At a hotel reception: What time does breakfast finish?

2.

At an airport: Where can I get a taxi to the city centre?

3.

In a new office building: Which floor is the IT department on?

4.

At a doctor’s surgery: How often does my son need to take this medicine?

5.

At a client’s office: When will the director be back from lunch?

6.

At an airport: Which gate does the flight to Tashkent leave from?

2. Phrases from our lessons

Write the phrase in English. Change its form to fit the sentence.
Example Everyone in the office had a coffee (выпил кофе) before the meeting.
1.

Our company (раньше работала) only in Russia, but now we have clients in Kazakhstan and Moldova.

2.

My children (раньше не любили) vegetables, but now they eat salad every day.

3.

In 2019 I (один раз был) in Tashkent for a conference.

4.

(навскидку), I’d say the new project will take about three months.

5.

Last week I (случайно встретил) an old university friend at the airport in Istanbul.

6.

A slow run in the park helps me (расслабиться) after a busy week.

7.

The new version of our app (стоит попробовать): it’s much faster.

8.

After our presentation, two banks from Uzbekistan (связались с) us.

9.

After a long flight I (лучше бы поехал) straight to the hotel than to the office.

10.

When I choose running shoes, (для меня важнее всего) comfort, not the price.
