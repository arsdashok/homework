1. Yes, that’s true, but…

Join the two ideas into one sentence with may …, but or may have …, but.
Example This laptop is heavy. but: the battery lasts all day → This laptop may be heavy, but the battery lasts all day.
The trip cost a lot. but: the children loved it → The trip may have cost a lot, but the children loved it.
