It depends on whether…

About 10 minutes.

1. Would you do it?

Answer in one sentence: I would / I wouldn’t …, but it depends on whether …
Example A concert in a big park on Friday. Tickets: 70 euros, no refunds. It ends at midnight. Would you buy tickets?
→ I would buy two tickets, but it depends on whether the last metro is after midnight.
1.

A half marathon in Lisbon is in six weeks. Entry costs 45 euros. Your longest run so far is 10 km. Would you sign up?

2.

Your elder son’s school offers a five-day ski trip for beginners in February. It costs 400 euros. Would you send him?

3.

A strong developer wants to join your company. She lives in Kazakhstan and wants to work from home. She asks for 20% more than your budget. Would you hire her?

4.

Friends invite you on a boat trip on Sunday: four hours at sea, 35 euros per person, the boat leaves at 8 a.m. Would you go?

5.

A new 600-page biography of Peter the Great comes out in English. It costs 28 euros. The English is quite academic. Would you buy it?

2. Choose the right words

Choose the words that fit each reply.
1.

A colleague offers you a lift home at six, when the roads are full.

“Thanks, but in rush hour I prefer (take / to take / for taking) the metro.”

2.

Your team is working late. Your partner asks: “The steak house round the corner, or pizza at the office?” You want to finish quickly.

“I’d rather (ordering pizza / to order pizza / order pizza) here at the office.”

3.

Your company once took part in a big conference in Amsterdam. It happened only one time.

“We (took part in / used to take part in / were taking part in) a big conference in Amsterdam.”

4.

Your friend Igor read his daughter a story every night when she was five. Now she reads by herself. Igor says:

“When she was small, I (use to read / used to reading / used to read) her a story every night.”
