Do you know what…?

1. Polite questions

Ask a polite question with the words in brackets. Start with Do you know or Could you tell me.
Example At the pier, to a man with a ticket: (how often / the boat to the island / leave) → Do you know how often the boat to the island leaves?
1.

2.

3.

4.

5.
