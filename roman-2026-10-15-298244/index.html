He ended up…

1. The plan and the unexpected result

Write one sentence: the plan, then what really happened. Use ended up. In 3, 4 and 5 the result is yours.
Example You went to the shop for milk. (buy / a new coffee machine) → I went to the shop for milk and ended up buying a new coffee machine.
1.

You went out for a 20-minute run. (run / 15 kilometres)

2.

Your friend wanted to read one chapter before bed. (read / the whole book)

3.

You invited one friend for dinner.

4.

A company tested your app for one week.

5.

You opened your laptop on Sunday to check one email.
