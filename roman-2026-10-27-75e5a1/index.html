1. Is it worth it?

Answer in one sentence: … is worth it / … isn't worth it, or is worth + -ing. Add your reason.
Example A cooking course in Italy? → A cooking course in Italy is worth it, because you learn from real chefs.
1.

Is it worth paying for business class on a two-hour flight?

2.

A smartwatch for your running?

3.

Is it worth queuing for 40 minutes to see a famous museum?

4.

A three-day public speaking course for your team?

2. Before…

Answer with used to + a verb.
Example Before e-books, what did you use to read on holiday? → I used to take two or three paper books.
1.

Before streaming, where did you find new films?

2.

Before smartphones, how did you use to remember phone numbers?

3.

When you were a student, what was a normal summer for you?

3. You like both

Answer, but don't choose one: either option is fine for you.
1.

«Do you prefer the sea or the mountains?»

2.

«A video call or a phone call with a new client?»
