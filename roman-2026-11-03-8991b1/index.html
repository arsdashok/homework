Would you mind…?

1. Ask politely

Write a polite request with Would you mind + -ing.
Example In a shop, the box you need is on the top shelf, and you can't reach it. → Would you mind getting it down for me?
1.

A plumber is coming at 9 a.m. tomorrow to fix your kitchen tap. You have an online call from 9 to 10. Ask him to come an hour later.

2.

You ordered a black backpack online. Today a grey one arrived. Write to the shop: you want a black one.

3.

At the gym, a man has left his bag on the only free bench, and you want to sit down for a minute.

4.

On a video call, a client is talking about a slide, but you can only see his face.

2. Your reply

Someone asks you. Write your reply in one or two sentences.
1.

Your neighbour says: “Would you mind watering my plants on Saturday and Sunday? I'll be away.” You're at home all weekend, and you're happy to help.

2.

A courier phones: “Would you mind collecting your parcel from the post office? It's 3 km from your house.” The parcel is a heavy printer, and you're at home all day.

3. A message to the hotel

Write 3 or 4 sentences to the hotel before you arrive.

You've booked a room in Vienna from 18 to 20 November for a conference. You have an online call at 8 a.m. every day.

In your message:

  • ask for the room you need, and say why (Would you mind + -ing)
  • ask politely what time breakfast starts
