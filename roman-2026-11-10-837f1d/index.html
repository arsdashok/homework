Showing a contrast

1. Although

Join each pair of facts in one sentence with although. For 2, 3 and 6, put although in the middle.
Example The hotel was cheap. The room was comfortable. → Although the hotel was cheap, the room was comfortable. In the middle: The room was comfortable, although the hotel was cheap.
