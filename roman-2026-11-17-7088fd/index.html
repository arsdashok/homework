Worth it, or no point?

1. Your decision

Is it worth doing, or is there no point? Write one sentence and give a reason.
Example Your friend wants to buy museum tickets online today. You're going on Sunday, and entry is free on Sundays. Should you buy them now? → There's no point in buying tickets: entry is free on Sundays.
1.

You're taking the train to the airport. The car park by the station costs 12 euros a day, and free parking is a 15-minute walk away. You have a heavy suitcase, and it's raining. Should you pay for the parking by the station?

2.

Your flight has been delayed by three hours. Your friend wants to leave for the airport right now. Should you leave now?

3.

It's going to rain all weekend. Should you take the car to the car wash on Saturday?

4.

You're trying out a new planning app. The free version works well, but the paid version, 6 euros a month, would save you about two hours every week. Should you pay for the paid version?

5.

A colleague suggests a negotiation course for your next meetings with clients: two evenings a week from January, first lesson free. Should you sign up for the whole course now?

6.

You're at a two-day conference. Breakfast at the hotel costs 25 euros, and the café next door does a good breakfast for 8 euros. Your colleague suggests having breakfast at the hotel. Should you?
