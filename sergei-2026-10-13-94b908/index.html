03

Short answers

Leila is an estate agent. You ask her these questions.

Choose Leila’s short answer.

Write: Yes, I do / No, I don’t / Yes, I am / No, I’m not / Yes, I can / No, I can’t / Yes, I did / No, I didn’t.

1. Do you work on Saturdays?“ I’m at the office every Saturday.”

2. Are you from Abu Dhabi?“ I’m from Dubai.”

3. Did you see the new villa yesterday?“ I was ill.”

4. Can you show us the house tomorrow?“ I’m free at ten.”

5. Do you have a car?“ I take a taxi everywhere.”
