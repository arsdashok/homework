01

Sort the questions

Drag each ending into the right box: ARE YOU or DO YOU.

Write each ending in the right box: ARE YOU or DO YOU.

Are you / Is …? before an adjective, a noun or a place.
hungry → Are you hungry?
Do you / Do they / Does …? before a verb (like, live, have).
like pizza → Do you like pizza?

On a phone: tap an ending, then tap a box. Tap an ending in a box to send it back.
