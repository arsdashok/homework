Our decimal strategies

  • × and ÷ by 10, 100, 1000: the digits move, the point stays. 3.4 × 10 = 34
  • Ignore the point: 0.3 × 42 → 3 × 42 = 126 → one place back: 12.6
  • A zero on the end still counts: 6 × 50 = 300, so 0.6 × 50 = 30.0 = 30
  • Estimate first: 0.3 is about a third, and a third of 42 is 14.
  • Start from an easy value: 0.8 = 1 − 0.2, so 0.8 × 15 = 15 − 3
  • Divide by 20, 30, 40, 50: 2000 ÷ 50 is ÷ 5, then ÷ 10
