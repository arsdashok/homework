Allusion, allegory or idiom

Is each one an allusion, an allegory or an idiom? Choose.

Allusion: a famous name used to describe someone or something else.

Allegory: a whole story whose characters and events stand for bigger ideas.

Idiom: a fixed phrase that means something different from its own words.
